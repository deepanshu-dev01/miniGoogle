🧮 PageRank Mathematics & Power Iteration

Larry Page and Sergey Brin modeled web browsing as a random surfer. A link from page A to page B is a vote of confidence. The score of page B is the sum of fractional votes from all pages linking to it.

1. The PageRank Equation

PR(u) = (1 - d)/N + d × ∑ [ PR(v) / L(v) ]

Where:
• u is the target page.
• N is the current total number of pages (6).
• d = 0.85 is the damping factor (85% link follower, 15% random teleport).
• v ∈ B(u) are pages linking directly into u.
• L(v) is the count of outbound links on page v.

2. Damping Factor Controller

The term (1 - d) / N is the minimum baseline authority granted to every page, even orphan spam with zero incoming backlinks.
